# Platforms

Each platform has one job. Cadence is a starting point sized for one person signing off; review it after four weeks. Make visuals once and resize; always rewrite captions per platform.

| Platform | Job for JOMO | Posts per week | Main formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Credibility with partners and funders | 3 | Impact posts, programme updates, founder and team insights, PDF carousels |
| Instagram | Youth reach and follower growth | 4 feed posts, stories most days | Reels, carousels, stories with polls and countdowns |
| Facebook | Community, families and events | 3 | Instagram visuals with longer captions, event pages |
| X | Conversation and news hooks | 5 short posts or 1 thread | Quick takes, threads, replies to climate news |

## Content pillars (monthly mix)

| Pillar | Covers | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Impact proof | Past clean-ups and tree planting, YCEF fellows' projects, results in numbers | 30% |
| Climate, explained locally | Explainers, myth-busting, policy-to-environment (apolitical) | 25% |
| Youth and volunteer voices | Spotlights, behind the scenes, JOMO's Pods clips | 20% |
| Opportunities and calls to action | YCEF, volunteering, events, new podcast episodes | 15% |
| Partners and ecosystem | Partner thank-yous, co-branded work, awareness days | 10% |

Fixed dates to plan around (verify each year): International Day of Education (24 Jan), World Water Day (22 Mar), Earth Day (22 Apr), World Environment Day (5 Jun), International Youth Day (12 Aug), World Cleanup Day (September), Nigeria's Independence Day (1 Oct), the UN climate conference (COP, usually November), plus "Happy new month" on the 1st and TGIF on Fridays.

## Sizes (pixels)

| Use | Size |
|---|---|
| Instagram and Facebook feed, LinkedIn feed, carousels | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) |
| Square fallback | 1080 × 1080 |
| Stories and reels | 1080 × 1920 (9:16); keep key content out of the top and bottom 250 |
| X and LinkedIn landscape | 1600 × 900 (16:9) |

## Caption rules

- **Instagram:** hook first line, short paragraphs, 3 to 5 hashtags at the end, "link in bio" for links.
- **Facebook:** can run longer; links work in the caption.
- **LinkedIn:** professional and evidence-led, first two lines must stand alone before "see more", 1 to 2 hashtags, tag partners by their page.
- **X:** under 280 characters; one idea; a link or a question.

## Scheduling (free first)

| Platform | Free option |
|---|---|
| Facebook and Instagram | Meta Business Suite planner (feed posts, reels, stories) |
| LinkedIn | LinkedIn's built-in scheduler when posting as the JOMO page |
| X | Calendar reminder, then post by hand (native scheduling may need a paid tier) |
| Backup for all | Buffer free plan (limited channels and queued posts; check current limits) |

The content calendar lives in the Google Sheet "JOMO Social Content Calendar" in the JOMO Social Drive folder. Status values: Idea, Drafting, In review, Approved, Scheduled, Posted.
