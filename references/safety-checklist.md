# Policy and safety review

Run every post through all checks before the operator sees it. Output one verdict per post:

- **PASS** — no issues.
- **FIX** — list each issue and the exact edit you made.
- **BLOCK** — explain the risk in one or two sentences; the operator decides.

This review lowers the risk of removals, reduced reach and account restrictions; it cannot guarantee a platform won't act. Platform rules change: if a session has web access, check the current Meta Community Standards, Meta's branded content and ads policies, X rules and LinkedIn Professional Community Policies once a quarter and tell the operator about any change that affects JOMO.

## Checks

1. **Accuracy.** Every statistic, date and claim has a source on file (from the operator, JOMO files or a cited page). No claim JOMO can't prove, including funding promises (YCEF has no funding guarantee and no pitch competition).
2. **Consent.** Everyone identifiable in a photo or video has consent on record (see the Consent log in the JOMO Social Drive folder). Anyone under 18: school or parent consent, and never pair a child's face with their name or school.
3. **Engagement bait.** Meta reduces the reach of posts that push people to tag, comment, react, share or vote ("Tag a friend who…", "Comment YES", "Share if you agree"). Rewrite as a genuine question or a clear call to action.
4. **Apolitical rule.** No sides, candidates, slogans or personal praise or attacks on officials (see `voice.md`).
5. **Boosting.** A paid boost about environmental policy may be treated by Meta as an ad about social issues or politics, which can need advertiser authorisation and a "Paid for by" disclaimer. Flag any post the operator plans to boost; unpaid posts are not affected.
6. **No shaming.** Never show identifiable people littering, in poor conditions or in distress. Show the problem, not a person to blame.
7. **Climate claims.** No exaggeration or unsourced predictions. Disputed claims can be labelled or limited by platforms.
8. **AI imagery.** A photorealistic AI-generated or heavily edited image is disclosed in the caption; never present it as a real JOMO activity.
9. **Copyright.** JOMO's own or properly licensed images; music only from the platform's own library; partner logos only with permission.
10. **Partners.** Paid or sponsored partner content uses the platform's branded content or paid partnership label. Partners approve before they are named in new content.
11. **Fundraising.** Donation asks link only to the website or a platform's own fundraising tools, with no promises about outcomes.
12. **Links and handles.** Every link opens and goes where it says; no unknown link shorteners; partner handles are correct.
13. **Hate, harassment and safety.** Nothing that mocks a group, community, religion or ethnicity; no stereotypes in humour; nothing that could expose a person's location or identity without consent.

## Comment and message replies

Flag to the operator, without replying, anything involving: politics or elections, a complaint about a named person, harassment, a safeguarding or child-protection concern, media or journalist requests, or legal threats.
