# JOMO avatars: Amara and Tobi (draft v0.1, 2026-10-24)
Role: Creative Director, with the Copywriter

Status: **draft personas, paused by the operator on 2026-10-24 until the Canva for Nonprofits licence is approved.** No art has been made yet. Nothing here goes out until the operator approves the personas and then each post.

## The idea

Two young Lagos-based friends who see the same climate problem from opposite ends. Amara wants to *do something* right now and get everyone to come. Tobi wants to *get it right* first and check the facts. Each post is the two of them meeting in the middle: the hook from Amara, the proof from Tobi, the action from both.

They are a duo, not two separate accounts. They contrast in energy, look and voice. They blend in through the JOMO colours they both wear, the same values and a shared sign-off.

## Rules for both (safety checklist item 8)

- **AI characters, always labelled.** "AI avatar" on screen every time, plus the caption line "Amara and Tobi are JOMO's AI characters." Switch on each platform's AI label.
- **Never presented as real.** They are not JOMO staff, volunteers, YCEF fellows or partners. They don't "attend" real JOMO events, give testimonials, or claim personal experiences of real places or results. Proof of impact uses JOMO's real photos.
- **Clearly adults.** Amara is 23 and Tobi is 24. Designs must read as adults (no school uniforms, no childlike proportions).
- **Not modelled on a real person.** Their faces and voices aren't based on any real person, influencer or public figure. If the art comes out resembling someone well known, redo it.
- **Apolitical.** No views on parties, candidates or officials, same as `voice.md`.
- **Traits aren't tied to ethnicity, religion or region.** Their states of origin are left unstated on purpose, so neither trait reads as a stereotype of a group.
- **Every fact they say is fact-checked** like a caption. All on-screen text is added in the edit, not by the image or video generator.

## Amara Okafor-Bello · "the one who makes things happen"

- **Age and role:** 23, female. A recent graduate who now runs small community projects and has a big following because she's always somewhere doing something.
- **Personality:** adventurous, bold, persuasive, a natural leader. Says yes first and figures it out on the way. Influential because she shows up, not because she shows off.
- **Relatable because:** she is the friend who drags the whole group chat to the beach clean-up, organises the area WhatsApp group, and makes a 30-second video out of everything.
- **Voice:** fast, warm, playful. Code-switches into Pidgin naturally ("Wetin we dey wait for?"). Short sentences. Questions that dare you.
- **Catchphrases:** "Who's coming?" · "Let's go see am." · "Small action, loud result."
- **Weakness (keeps her real):** rushes ahead and sometimes gets a fact wrong. Tobi catches it.
- **Look (art brief):** dark skin, short natural hair or braids pulled up under a lime headwrap. Oversized Leaf Lime shirt or JOMO tee, cargo trousers, sneakers, a crossbody bag, sometimes a phone on a mini tripod. Open, moving poses: walking, pointing, mid-laugh.
- **Colour accent:** Leaf Lime (#A5CD39) as the main colour, with Ink.
- **Covers:** calls to action, events, YCEF promos, challenges, "come with me" formats.

## Tobi Adeyemi-Musa · "the one who checks the receipts"

- **Age and role:** 24, male. A master's student in environmental science who works part time in a lab. He reads the whole report, not just the headline.
- **Personality:** nerdy, careful, follows the rules and likes them because rules keep people safe. Dry, quiet humour. Loyal.
- **Relatable because:** he is the friend who reads the terms and conditions, forwards the NiMet alert to the family group, and asks "Who said so?" when a rumour comes in.
- **Voice:** calm, precise, plain English with the odd dry joke. Always names his source. Explains jargon in one line.
- **Catchphrases:** "Source, please." · "Let me check." · "Steps first, then speed."
- **Weakness (keeps him real):** overthinks and waits too long. Amara pushes him to act.
- **Look (art brief):** medium-dark skin, low fade, round glasses. Ink or Off-white button-up shirt over a JOMO tee, a lime lanyard, sometimes a clipboard, a notebook or a tablet showing a chart. Steadier poses: hand raised to make a point, holding up a document.
- **Colour accent:** Ink (#1E2220) and Off-white, with Olive (#77962A).

## How they blend

- Both always wear something from the JOMO palette, and the JOMO sprout pin is on both outfits.
- The same illustrated style, line weight and lighting, so they look like one world.
- A shared sign-off: Amara: "Who's coming?" Tobi: "And bring your facts." Both: "JOMO."
- They disagree on *how*, never on *whether*. Both care about their street.

## Formats that use the contrast

1. **"Amara vs. Tobi":** the same question, two takes (e.g. "Heatwave this week: what do we do?"). Amara: action. Tobi: the facts behind it. They end on one agreed step.
2. **"Source, please":** Amara shares a viral climate claim, and Tobi checks it live (myth-busting pillar).
3. **"Who's coming?":** Amara invites people to a real JOMO event. The event photos stay real; the avatar only presents.
4. **Explainer carousels:** each slide has a small Amara or Tobi sticker reacting.

## Art direction for the tools

- **Style:** flat-colour illustration with soft shading, a modern Afro-futurist feel, and bold outlines that match JOMO's flyers. Not photorealistic, so they can't be mistaken for real people.
- **Make once, reuse:** a character sheet for each (front, three-quarter, 4 expressions, 3 poses), then reuse those exact images instead of re-generating them each time.
- **Canva prompt, Amara:** "Flat vector illustration of a confident 23-year-old Nigerian woman, dark skin, braids under a lime-green headwrap, oversized lime-green shirt, cargo trousers, sneakers, crossbody bag, walking and pointing forward, mid-laugh, plain off-white background, bold outlines, soft shading, adult proportions, no text."
- **Canva prompt, Tobi:** "Flat vector illustration of a thoughtful 24-year-old Nigerian man, medium-dark skin, low fade haircut, round glasses, dark button-up shirt over a t-shirt, green lanyard, holding a tablet with a simple chart, one hand raised making a point, plain off-white background, bold outlines, soft shading, adult proportions, no text."
- **Voice:** CapCut text-to-speech, only voices marked "Commercial use", with Nigerian-English voices if offered. Check that both say "Y-C-E-F" and "NiMet" correctly.
