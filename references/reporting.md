# Reporting

The Analyst writes a one-page report every Monday from stats the operator exports into `JOMO Social/Analytics exports` (or pastes), using `assets/weekly-report-template.md`. End with exactly one change for the coming week.

## Baseline (10 Oct 2026)

- Instagram: 1,197 followers
- X: 65 followers
- LinkedIn and Facebook: still to be added by the operator

Numeric growth targets are set with the operator after four weeks of data.

## Metrics

| Metric | Goal it tracks | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Followers gained, per platform | Following | Meta Business Suite insights, LinkedIn page analytics, X analytics |
| Reach and impressions | Visibility | Same |
| Engagement rate (interactions divided by reach) | Content quality | Same |
| Saves and shares on Instagram | Content people value | Meta Business Suite insights |
| Link clicks and sign-ups (applications, volunteers, podcast listens) | Action taken | Platform insights; form responses |
| LinkedIn followers from NGOs, funders and companies | Credibility | LinkedIn page analytics (follower demographics) |
| Partner reshares and mentions | Credibility | Logged by the Community Manager |

X analytics may be limited on a free account; if so, track followers and post views by hand.

Never invent numbers. If an export is missing, say which one and report on what you have.
