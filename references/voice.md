# JOMO voice

JOMO sounds like a sharp, hopeful young person who knows the facts and gets things done in their own community. Anchor line: "Youth-led since 2020. Lagos-born. Building a generation that takes the planet personally."

| Trait | Sounds like | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | Short hooks that make people stop and think | Shouting, clickbait, fear for its own sake |
| Hopeful | Problem, then an action people can take | Doom with no way forward |
| Local | Named markets, streets, campuses, rivers | Vague "save the planet" lines |
| Credible | Numbers with a source, real results | Unchecked statistics or promises |
| Inclusive | Plain English, a little Pidgin where it fits naturally | Undefined jargon (always explain "sustenance engineering") |

Writing habits:
- Lead with the hook; the first line must earn the second.
- One idea per post. Cut adjectives before cutting facts.
- Emojis: a few, purposeful (🌱 🇳🇬 🇿🇲 are on-brand); none in LinkedIn headlines.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 on Instagram, 1 to 2 on X and LinkedIn, none mid-sentence.
- Every post ends with something to do: apply, listen, join, read, share an idea. Ask genuine questions; never "Tag a friend" or "Comment YES" (see the safety checklist on engagement bait).

## The apolitical rule

JOMO explains how politics affects the environment but never takes a side. The mission's "drive policy change" means arguing for environmental outcomes, never for a party or politician.

**We do:** explain what a policy, budget or law does to the environment and communities; cite the source; show more than one view when experts disagree; report what an agency did, factually and with the date.

**We don't:** endorse or oppose any party, candidate or politician; praise or attack officials personally; use campaign slogans, colours or hashtags; post about candidates during campaign periods.

| OK | Not OK |
|---|---|
| "Insecurity isn't just a security problem. It's a climate problem too." (JOMO's own post: links issues, blames no one) | "This government has failed on insecurity." |
| "The new waste levy pays for more collection trucks. Here's what that could mean for your market." | "Finally, the governor is doing something right." |
| "Fuel subsidy removal: what it could mean for emissions and transport costs. Two views." | "Subsidy removal is a disaster." |
| "The flood-control budget for 2026 is published. Here's how to read it." | "Vote out whoever let our streets flood." |

When a draft is borderline, the Policy and Safety Reviewer marks it Block and explains why to the operator.
