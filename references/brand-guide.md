# JOMO brand guide (v1.0, October 2026)

## Who JOMO is

JOMO Climate Change Initiative: youth-led since 2020, Lagos-born, building a generation that takes the planet personally. Tagline: "Raising eco-conscious generations." Website: www.jomoclimatechange.org. The handle is @jomoclimate (confirmed by the operator, 2026-10-23); use it on all graphics.

**Mission:** We empower young leaders to raise eco-conscious generations by harnessing every sphere of influence (education, media, government, business, community, arts and science) to advance sustainable practices, drive policy change and inspire global action for a resilient planet.

**Vision:** A sustainable Earth handed down to future generations: well-educated, eco-conscious and empowered to thrive in harmony with nature through youth-led innovation and stewardship.

**Values:** Integrity. Inclusivity. Sustainability. Action. We lead by example and empower others to do the same.

Every post should serve at least one of these.

## Programmes and partners

- **Running now:** JOMO's Pods (the podcast) and the Young Climate Educators Fellowship (YCEF). YCEF applications open Mon 12 Oct 2026 and close Mon 26 Oct 2026; Weekend 1 is Fri 13 Nov 2026. YCEF is a 3-weekend virtual programme for 50 fellows across Nigeria and Zambia, open to university students and grassroots community volunteers aged 18 and over. Fellows research hyper-local climate challenges, design youth-led "sustenance engineering" projects and learn to pitch for seed funding. Never promise funding: there is no funding guarantee and no pitch competition.

## Colours

| Olive | #77962A | Logo wordmark green; Instagram headline green; accent text on light |
| Charcoal | #323534 | Logo arc and tagline |
| Ink | #1E2220 | Dark backgrounds (stands in for the website's #0A0A0A); text on lime or light |
| Off-white | #F4F6EC | Light backgrounds; text on dark |

Contrast rules: Lime and Off-white text work on Ink; Ink text works on Lime and Off-white. Never put Olive text on Ink for body copy (too dark), and never put the full-colour logo directly on Lime.

## Type

- **Everyday headlines:** tall, condensed, bold capitals in white with one line in olive green, as on JOMO's Instagram. Anton or Bebas Neue are close free matches.
- **Campaign headlines:** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, for campaigns that need to stand apart (used for YCEF).
- **Body and labels:** Lato (the logo typeface). Labels in bold capitals with wide letter spacing.
- If a face isn't available, use the closest heavy sans-serif and tell the operator.

## Logo

- Full-colour logo (`assets/logos/jomo-logo.png`) on white or very light backgrounds.
- Light version (`assets/logos/jomo-logo-light.png`, white arc and text) on Ink or Charcoal.
- On Lime, place the logo on a white rounded tile.
- Never recolour the leaves, stretch it or add effects. Clear space at least the height of the "J".

## Design signatures

- Short, bold hooks that challenge the reader ("Insecurity isn't just a security problem. It's a climate problem too." / "Your street has a climate problem. You've got the fix.").
- Photo-led layouts: one person or scene fills the frame, headline over it.
- A handle bar at the foot with the Instagram and LinkedIn icons and @jomoclimate.
- Recurring moments: "Happy new month", TGIF, national days, environmental days.
- Strikethrough reframes ("~~Climate anxiety.~~ Climate action."), tilted tape banners, round stamps and stickers, the two-leaf sprout as a large cropped shape.
- Redaction and countdown teasers before launches; dark and lime slides alternating in carousels.

## Layout rules (learned the hard way)

- Decorative shapes (leaves, stickers, tape) sit in empty space and never behind or touching text.
- No headline word left alone on its last line; break lines on purpose.
- Nothing important within the top and bottom 250px of a 9:16 story (app bars cover it).
- Text inside banners or tape must be fully visible, never sliced by the frame edge.
- Check every design at full size before presenting it.

## Photography

- Real people and real places first: volunteers in branded tees, tree planting, community work. These build the most trust with partners and funders.
- Composite or AI-generated images, AI video and AI avatars are for explainers, concepts and promos, never as evidence of JOMO's work. Label them on screen and in the caption (safety checklist item 8).
- Action over posing. Name the place in the caption.
- Only photos with consent on record; never pair a child's face with their name or school.
