# Canva Brand Kit setup for JOMO

Everything to type into Canva's Brand Kit, taken from `references/brand-guide.md`. Only the team's Owner, an Admin or a Brand Designer can create or edit a Brand Kit. The account connected to Claude is a Member, so the operator (as Owner) does the setup.

## Steps
1. In Canva, open **Brand** in the left menu, then **Add new** (or **Create a Brand Kit**). Name it **JOMO Climate Change Initiative**.
2. Rename or delete the unnamed kit and "Metalira" if they aren't JOMO's, so nobody uses the wrong one.
3. Fill in each section below.

## Logos
Upload both files from `assets/logos/`:
- `jomo-logo.png`: full-colour, for white or light backgrounds.
- `jomo-logo-light.png`: white arc and text, for dark (Ink or Charcoal) backgrounds.

Rules: never recolour the leaves, stretch the logo or add effects. On Lime, put the logo on a white rounded tile.

## Colours (palette name: "JOMO")
| Name | Hex | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Leaf Lime | #A5CD39 | Main accent: highlights, buttons, stickers |
| Leaf Green | #8FB73C | Second leaf tone, shading |
| Olive | #77962A | Headline green on light backgrounds |
| Charcoal | #323534 | Logo arc and tagline |
| Ink | #1E2220 | Dark backgrounds, and text on Lime or Off-white |
| Off-white | #F4F6EC | Light backgrounds, and text on Ink |

Safe text pairs: Lime or Off-white text on Ink; Ink text on Lime or Off-white. Never Olive body text on Ink.

## Fonts (Brand fonts, in Canva's "Text styles")
| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Title (everyday headlines) | **Anton** | Tall, condensed, all capitals |
| Subtitle (campaign headlines, e.g. YCEF) | **Bricolage Grotesque**, ExtraBold | If it isn't in Canva's library, upload the TTF from fonts.google.com (free licence) |
| Body and labels | **Lato** | Regular for body text, Black in capitals with wide spacing for labels |

## Brand voice (Canva's "Brand voice" box, if it's offered)
JOMO sounds like a sharp, hopeful young person who knows the facts and gets things done in their own community. Bold hooks, then a clear action. Plain English, a little Pidgin where it fits. Every number has a source. Never political, never doom with no way forward. Youth-led since 2020. Lagos-born. "Raising eco-conscious generations."
