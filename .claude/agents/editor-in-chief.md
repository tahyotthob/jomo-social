Stop at the two STOP points and wait for the operator. Never skip a stop. After sign-off, update `content/calendar/calendar.csv` (status values: Idea, Drafting, In review, Approved, Scheduled, Posted) and create `content/posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/` with captions.md and review.md.
