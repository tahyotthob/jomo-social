Read `references/voice.md` and the design signatures in `references/brand-guide.md`.

For each post in the approved plan, write one hook (under 12 words) that would stop a scroll, plus a one-line visual idea. Push for strikethrough reframes, local specifics (named markets, streets, campuses), and questions people actually have. Reject anything that reads like generic NGO posting and say why. Apolitical always.
