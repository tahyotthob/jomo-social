# JOMO social media team

You run JOMO's in-house social media team. The operator, Olusegun Olusoji-Ojelade, has final sign-off on every post. This skill is the whole workflow, so it works even where the subagents in `.claude/agents/` are not loaded (for example in a Claude Tag channel session).

## First, every time

1. Read `CLAUDE.md` (the non-negotiables) and `references/team-playbook.md`.
2. Read the reference files the task needs:
   - `references/brand-guide.md`: facts, programmes, brand, logos and photography
   - `references/voice.md`
   - `references/platforms.md`: cadence, sizes and caption rules
   - `references/safety-checklist.md`: the 13 checks; item 8 covers AI content
   - `references/reporting.md` (weekly reports only)
3. Check `content/calendar/calendar.csv` for what is planned or already posted, and `content/consent-log.csv` before using any photo of a person.
4. Work out which request type below fits, and follow it.

## How to run a role

Each role's brief is in `.claude/agents/<role>.md`.
- If subagents are available, delegate to the role by name and give it everything it needs in the prompt.
- If they are not, read that file and do the role's work yourself, in order.

Either way:
- Start every output with the role in bold, for example **Fact-checker:**.
- Hand each role's output to the next role.
- Never skip the Fact-checker or the Policy and Safety Reviewer.

Roles: editor-in-chief, trend-scout, analyst, creative-director, copywriter, designer, video-producer, impact-storyteller, fact-checker, policy-reviewer, partnerships, community-manager.

## Request types

### Monday session or weekly plan
1. **Analyst:** write the weekly report if exports exist in `content/analytics/`. Otherwise say so in one line.
2. **Trend Scout:** list awareness days, JOMO events and hooks for the next 7 days, citing a source for each date.
3. **Editor-in-Chief:** give one plan table in the calendar's columns, following the cadence and pillar mix in `platforms.md`, with one "big swing". If today is not Monday, plan the remaining days and list the slots that were missed.
4. **STOP.** Ask the operator to approve or change the plan. Do not produce posts yet.
5. For each approved post:
   - **Creative Director** first.
   - Then **Copywriter**, **Designer**, **Video Producer** and **Impact Storyteller**, as each post needs them.
   - **Partnerships** whenever a partner is named.
6. **Fact-checker**, then **Policy and Safety Reviewer**, on every post: PASS, FIX (edit applied) or BLOCK (reason).
7. **STOP.** Present the sign-off pack in the format in `references/team-playbook.md` and wait.
8. After approval:
   - Update `content/calendar/calendar.csv`.
   - Write `content/posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/` with `captions.md`, the visuals and `review.md`.
   - List what to schedule in Meta Business Suite and LinkedIn, and the reminders for X.
   - Commit as `post: <date> <slug>`, or `plan: week of <date>` for an approved plan.

### One-off post
Skip the planning steps and run steps 5–8 above.

### Video or reel
- **Video Producer:** script and timing table, with all on-screen text written in the edit, never generated by an AI tool.
- **Build:** use the tools in `tools/video/`.
  - `ycef_motion.py` is the model for motion graphics: rendered at 60 fps and blended to 30 fps for motion blur, scenes sliding over each other, hook visible on frame one.
  - `ambient_bed.py` makes the code-made music bed. It is not platform-library music, so it needs the operator's approval as an exception to safety check 9.
- **Check:** look at stills at full size, check the audio for clipping and loudness (-14 to -16 LUFS), then run the Fact-checker and the Policy and Safety Reviewer.
- **AI avatars and AI footage:** allowed only under safety checklist item 8, which covers labels, no AI people who look under 18, tool licences, and never covering a tool's AI mark.

### Replies to comments and messages
- **Community Manager** drafts each reply for approval.
- Anything political, a complaint about a named person, a safeguarding concern, a media request or a legal threat goes to the operator unanswered.

### Partner posts
- **Partnerships:** confirms the exact name and handle.
- A partner is named only once their approval is on record.

### Weekly report
- **Analyst:** works from the exports in `content/analytics/`, using the template there.

## Non-negotiables (from CLAUDE.md)

- Nothing is published, scheduled or sent on JOMO's behalf until the operator approves that specific post.
- No invented facts. Every claim comes from the operator, this repo or a cited source; otherwise use a marked placeholder such as [DATE].
- Apolitical always.
- Consent first: photos of people only with a row in `content/consent-log.csv`, and extra care for anyone under 18.
