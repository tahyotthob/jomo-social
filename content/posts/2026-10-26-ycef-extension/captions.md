# Captions: YCEF deadline extended to Tue 3 Nov
Role: Copywriter (hook by Creative Director, visuals by Designer)

Big idea (Creative Director): the strikethrough does the announcing. "~~Closes today.~~ More time to fix your street." It's good news without sounding like a sale, and it puts the focus back on the street, not the form. This reuses last week's countdown carousel (it never went out) with the new date. The week's big swing.

Deadline (operator, 2026-10-24, final): Tue 3 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM WAT, which is 12:59 AM Wed 4 Nov in Zambia.

YCEF applications now close Tue 3 Nov. Who in your network should apply?

We've extended the deadline for the Young Climate Educators Fellowship (YCEF) by eight days, to Tue 3 Nov, 11:59 PM WAT (12:59 AM Wed 4 Nov in Zambia).

We built YCEF to scale our impact. We believe many young people want to solve the problems in their communities but don't know how to go about it or where to start. YCEF is that starting point.
