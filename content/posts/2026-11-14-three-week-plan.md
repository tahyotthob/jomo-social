## Design: less "AI-looking", more professional (Designer)

What makes the current posters read as AI or template-made:
- **Generic stock-photo look:** the flood and plastic stock photos.
- **Flat code-drawn shapes:** sprouts and squiggles.
- **Too much text:** every slide is text-led.
- **One layout repeated:** the same pattern on every post.

The changes for this run:
1. **Real JOMO photos first, larger.** The handbook's gallery photos (Ketu Market with Oxygen X, schools, "Save Our Future") need consent rows, and Oxygen X's approval for the Ketu set, before use.
2. **One proper template system, built in Canva** (JOMO has the Nonprofit licence) as a brand kit:
   - fixed grid and margins;
   - two type families only;
   - photo frames;
   - a "series" header for each of the three series.

   Every post in a series then looks the same and recognisably JOMO, rather than one-off. The Canva plugin (offered to the operator) adds design feedback, a brand check and resizing.
3. **Less text per slide:**
   - one idea per slide;
   - a maximum of about 20 words on the image;
   - detail goes in the caption.
4. **Subtler texture and detail:** real paper grain, photo grading in the brand palette, and consistent cut-out shadows. No decorative clip-art leaves on every slide.
5. **Typographic hierarchy:** a strong headline, a small label and generous white space.
