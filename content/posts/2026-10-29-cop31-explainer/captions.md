Caption — LinkedIn:
COP31 is in 11 days. Here's what it is, in 5 slides.

COP stands for "Conference of the Parties": the countries (and the European Union) that are Parties to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change. They meet once a year to agree how the world responds to climate change.

COP31 runs from 9 to 20 November 2026 in Antalya, Türkiye.

Why it matters beyond the conference hall: the targets and finance countries agree, for cutting emissions and for adapting to a hotter climate, shape how places like Lagos and Lusaka prepare for heat and floods.

We'll explain what gets decided, and what it could mean for Nigeria and Zambia, in plain language.

What would you most like explained about COP31?
