# Captions: Happy new month, welcome to November
Role: Copywriter (hook by Creative Director, visuals by Designer)

Big idea (Creative Director): the operator's own new-month format (giant month name, a real person cut out in front, handle bar), made louder and younger:
- "October" crossed out;
- a tilted "Happy new month" tape;
- hand-drawn sparkles;
- three real JOMO volunteers instead of a stock model.

It replaces the plain "2 days left" story and X post (operator, 2026-11-01). The YCEF countdown rides along in the caption and on the story.

Photo: `oniwaya-cleanup-10.jpg` (three JOMO volunteers at the Oniwaya clean-up, Agege, Lagos), background removed with rembg. The cut-out is in `assets/photos/cutouts/`.
