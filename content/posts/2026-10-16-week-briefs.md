## Rejected ideas
- "Everyone deserves a safe home!" / "Let's end hunger together." Generic NGO register, no place or action.
- "Food security is a human right" / "Housing is a right." Policy advocacy; no one to act. Nothing about who should build or fix housing.
- "Tag someone who needs a safe home" / "Comment YES if you've seen a flood." Engagement bait.
- "Apply now, funding awaits!" / "Win seed funding." Breaks the never-promise-funding rule.
- "Don't miss out" countdowns without a concrete benefit.
