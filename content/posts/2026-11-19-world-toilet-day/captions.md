Caption — LinkedIn:
Today is World Toilet Day, and the theme is "Safe toilets. Equal futures."

In cities like Lagos, sanitation and climate meet at the drain. When heavy rain floods blocked drains, floodwater can mix with sewage, spreading diarrhoea and other diseases. Households without safe toilets carry most of the risk.

Clearing drains, keeping waste and sewage out of them, and handwashing after floods are small actions with real health benefits. They're also the kind of community action our Young Climate Educators Fellowship fellows are learning to lead.

Who checks the drains on your street before the rains?
