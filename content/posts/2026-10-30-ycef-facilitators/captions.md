Caption — LinkedIn:
Meet the facilitators leading Weekend 1 of the Young Climate Educators Fellowship (YCEF), which starts Fri 13 Nov.

Dr. Akpem Terese Shadrach is a veterinarian, social entrepreneur and founder of Vet Konect, a digital animal health platform for livestock farmers and pet owners across Africa. He has trained and mentored 30,000+ young people in digital skills, leads Jobberman Nigeria's facilitators in Benue State, and is a 2023 Mandela Washington Fellow.

Gome Ng’ona is a Zambian cultural heritage practitioner, development professional and youth advocate. His work spans youth-led climate action, malaria elimination advocacy and environmental sustainability, and he is passionate about climate education and storytelling.

Muhammad Daniyal Kamal is a climate advocate and sustainability leader from Pakistan with a Bachelor's in Environmental Sciences from Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad. He co-founded RE-AD: Resilience and Adaptation, a youth-led organisation for climate education, disaster risk reduction and water stewardship among students across Pakistan, and represented Pakistan as an official delegate at COP16 of the UN Convention to Combat Desertification in Riyadh.

YCEF brings together university students and grassroots community volunteers (18+) from Nigeria and Zambia over three virtual weekends to research hyper-local climate challenges and design youth-led solutions.
