Caption — Facebook:
Your street has a climate problem. You've got the fix. 🌱

"Someone should fix it" becomes "I can" with the Young Climate Educators Fellowship (YCEF).

Think of that blocked drain, that dump behind the market, that flooded corner. Fellows pick a problem like that on their own street and design a youth-led project for it.

What fellows do:
- Research one hyper-local climate challenge.
- Design a youth-led "sustenance engineering" project: a local fix built to keep running.
- Learn to pitch for seed funding. There is no funding guarantee.

Who can apply: university students and grassroots community volunteers aged 18 and over, in Nigeria 🇳🇬 and Zambia 🇿🇲.
The programme: 50 fellows, 3 virtual weekends. Weekend 1 is Fri 13 Nov 2026.

Applications close Mon 26 Oct, [TIME] WAT. That is 5 days from today.

Apply here: https://jomoclimatechange.org/events?apply=ycef
Know a student or community volunteer who should see this? Send them the link.
