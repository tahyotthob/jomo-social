# Captions: ~~A classroom subject.~~ It's your street. (big swing)
Role: Copywriter (hook by Creative Director, visuals by Designer)

Big idea (Creative Director): COP31's Sat 14 Nov theme is Children, Youth, Education and Skills. We turn a dry agenda item into a youth claim: climate education isn't only a school subject, it's what you can see on your own street. The proof is JOMO's own volunteers.

Photos are JOMO's own, with consent on record:
- `oniwaya-cleanup-04.jpg` (volunteers with a "Stop Littering, Start Recycling" sign outside a school building).
- `birthday-cleanup-2021-09.jpg` (volunteers with "The drainage is not a waste bin" signs).

The Rotary tree-planting photos are not used, because Rotary hasn't approved being named.
