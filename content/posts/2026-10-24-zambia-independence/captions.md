# Captions: Zambia Independence Day
Role: Copywriter (hook by Creative Director, visuals by Designer)

Big idea (Creative Director): the green on Zambia's flag stands for its farms and lush land. On its 62nd Independence Day, JOMO celebrates the young Zambians who keep it green. It's a celebration of the people and the land, not of any government or leader.

Visual note (Designer): JOMO has no photos from Zambia, so this is a graphic. It uses the flag's four colours as a band down the right edge, not a drawing of the flag (the eagle is left out on purpose rather than drawn badly).
