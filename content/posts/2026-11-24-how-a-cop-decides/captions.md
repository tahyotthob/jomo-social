How does a UN climate conference actually agree anything?

At a COP, negotiators work for two weeks on draft texts. A draft only becomes a decision once countries adopt it together at the closing plenary. Every line has to be acceptable to the countries in the room, which is why final sessions often run long.

COP31's final decisions are still being published. When they are, we'll share a plain-language summary of what was agreed and what it could mean for Nigeria and Zambia. The next conference, COP32, is due in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, 8–19 November 2027.
