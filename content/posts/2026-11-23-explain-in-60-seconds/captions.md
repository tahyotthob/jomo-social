Caption — Instagram:
Can you explain climate change to your neighbour in 60 seconds? 🌱 This weekend, YCEF fellows practised teaching climate. Here's a script anyone can use:

⏱ 0–15s: The sun warms the Earth. Some of that heat should escape back into space.
⏱ 15–30s: Burning coal, oil and gas adds gases that trap the heat, like a thicker blanket.
⏱ 30–45s: So we get more extremes: hotter days, heavier rain on blocked drains, longer dry spells on farms.
⏱ 45–60s: And we can still act: waste less, keep drains clear, plant trees, and talk about it.

Now try it on someone today 👇
