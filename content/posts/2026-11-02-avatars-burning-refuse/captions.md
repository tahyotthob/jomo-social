Caption — Facebook:
"Burning the refuse heap is the cleanest way to clear it." 🔥

Many of us grew up hearing this. Our AI characters Tobi and Amara put it to the test.

What the World Health Organization says: the smoke from burning waste carries air pollutants, some of which harm both health and the climate. Breathing it, whether for a short time or over a long time, can lead to coughing, skin irritation and respiratory diseases.

So the heap gets smaller, but the smoke goes into the air your family and neighbours breathe.

What to do instead:
- Bag your refuse and keep it covered until it's collected.
- Keep plastics, tyres and nylon out of any fire.
- Agree with your neighbours on one collection point.

Source: WHO, "Open waste burning: sectoral solutions for air pollution and health" (July 2025): https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/B09367
