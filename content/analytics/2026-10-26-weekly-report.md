## What worked
- **The story with a link sticker had the widest reach.** "Stop scrolling" reached 76 accounts, more than any feed post. Stories with a clear link get seen.
- **Carousels people swipe through get repeat views.** "5 reasons to apply" had 121 views from 24 viewers, about 5 views each, so people swiped through or came back. It also got 4 reposts.
- **The redact-then-reveal teaser pair worked.** "Unredacted" had the most likes (5) and 4 reposts.
- **Feelings first, then facts.** "Climate anxiety? Hold that thought." reached 57 accounts, second only to the story.

## What didn't
- **No follower growth.** Reach of 148 against a base of about 1,197 followers (baseline from 10 Oct) means most followers aren't seeing posts, and new viewers aren't following.
- **Almost no comments** (0 on every post), and only 0 or 1 share per post.
- **Morning posting.** Most posts went out at 09:00 to 11:00 WAT, but followers are most active around 6 PM, and on Mondays from 6 to 9 PM.
